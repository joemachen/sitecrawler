# ADR-0000: Title in the imperative

**Status:** Proposed
**Date:** YYYY-MM-DD

## Context

What situation forces a decision? What constraints apply? Two or three
sentences; link to issues or docs for detail.

## Decision

What we are doing, stated plainly.

## Consequences

What becomes easier, what becomes harder, what we give up. Include the
alternatives considered in one line each and why they lost.
